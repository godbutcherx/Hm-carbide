Frequently asked questions

Carbide rods are blanks made of tungsten carbide (WC) with a cobalt binder. They are the raw material for solid carbide cutting tools such as drills, end mills, and reamers used in CNC machining.

Our standard grades are HM 12Co (88% WC, 12% Co, 0.4 µm grain, 92.5 HRA), harder and more wear-resistant thanks to its ultrafine grain, and HM 10Co (90% WC, 10% Co, 0.8 µm grain, 91.8 HRA), tougher and more fracture-resistant. We also offer the grades HM12F (ultrafine, 0.4 µm), HM10S (fine, 0.6 µm) and H10F (medium, 0.8 µm).

Solid rods are standard blanks. Coolant channel rods have an internal bore (Ø 0.5–2.0 mm) that delivers coolant directly to the cutting edge — ideal for deep-hole drilling with longer tool life.
