Cut-to-length rods and custom lengths

When blanks cut to length pay off, and when the 330 mm rod is the better choice.

15 November 2026 · HM Carbide Swiss GmbH

Carbide rods are supplied in 330 mm lengths as standard. Toolmakers producing in series usually cut these rods to blank length themselves. Cut-to-length rods take that step off your hands: they arrive in the length you need for grinding.

Why cutting carbide takes effort

Carbide cannot be sawn like steel. Cutting it needs diamond cut-off wheels, a suitable machine and coolant. Every cut costs time, wheel wear and material, because the kerf is lost. At high volumes this adds up quickly.

When cut rods are the better choice

  • Series production with fixed lengths: if you grind the same tools in the same length again and again, pre-cut blanks save a whole operation.
  • No cutting machine of your own: without a diamond cut-off machine, the cut rod is the direct route to a grinding blank.
  • Predictable material use: you buy exactly the blanks you process, with no offcuts.
  • Faster start: the blanks go straight into the grinding machine.

When the 330 mm rod makes more sense

  • You make many different lengths in small batches.
  • You have your own cutting machine with spare capacity.
  • You want to react flexibly to short-notice orders and keep material in stock.

Custom lengths and sizes

If no standard size fits, we produce cut rods to your specification. To quote quickly, please tell us:

  • diameter and required blank length
  • the grade, if already decided (otherwise the tool type and workpiece material)
  • quantity and required delivery date
  • whether the rod needs a coolant channel

Tip: plan the blank length with an allowance for machining the end faces. If you tell us the length of the finished tool, we are happy to agree the blank length with you.
