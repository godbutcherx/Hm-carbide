What Data We Collect

When you visit our website, the following technical data is automatically stored in server log files:

  • IP address (immediately anonymised)
  • Date and time of access
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited

This data is used solely for technical security and anonymous statistics. No personal user profiles are created.

Cookies & Google Analytics

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored in your browser. Some are required for basic functions (e.g. language preference).

We use Google Analytics for anonymised usage statistics (Google Ireland Limited, Dublin). IP anonymisation is enabled — your IP is truncated within the EEA before being sent to Google. You can disable cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

We use Google’s Consent Mode. Without your consent, Google Analytics sets no cookies and only sends anonymous signals without identifiers. Analytics cookies are set only after you consent in the cookie notice. You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.
